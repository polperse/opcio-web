Our Method

A People-Centred Process

Four phases, one common thread: you and your team at the centre of every decision.

Scroll horizontally to see the 4 phases.

Initial phase

Phase 1 / 4

01

Diagnosis and Co-creation

We listen actively and analyse the context with a systemic lens. We involve every relevant voice to understand what is really happening and where the opportunity for change lies.

Results achieved

  • Map of the current state and stakeholders
  • Opportunities prioritised by impact and effort
  • Objectives agreed with the leadership team

Planning

Phase 2 / 4

02

Tailored Design

We design a unique solution with you, never an off-the-shelf package. We apply proven methodologies adapted to your context, pace and organisational culture.

Results achieved

  • Action plan with milestones and owners
  • Methodology adapted to your team
  • Success indicators and measurement system

Execution

Phase 3 / 4

03

Implementation and Support

We roll up our sleeves and get hands-on. We facilitate, train and support the change with continuous presence. Adjustments are part of the plan, not the exception.

Results achieved

  • Tailored sessions, workshops and training
  • Team and individual coaching
  • Periodic reviews and plan adjustments

Results

Phase 4 / 4

04

Impact Assessment

We measure what has really changed, not just what was done. We close the cycle with evidence and open the next step with clarity.

Results achieved

  • Impact report with key metrics
  • Learnings and practical recommendations
  • Roadmap for the next improvement cycle
